Free camera, everywhere. Drag to move, pinch to zoom from the whole island down to the street, twist to turn all the way around, two fingers up or down to tilt. The compass shows which way you face; tap it to face north again. "Watch" starts watch mode (right).

Follow anyone. Zoom in and tap a person, car or ship. The camera follows them, and a card shows who they are, what they're doing now and their day: home, work, shopping, home. Kai lives in the same house and walks the same road every day, because every citizen is a real person in the simulation.

The morning ferry from the Mainland brings six new citizens to Palm Cove.

Watch mode. All buttons fade away and the camera drifts slowly by itself. It visits whatever is happening: the morning ferry docking, people walking off with their bags, rush hour at the market, trucks at the windmill. A short line says what you're seeing. Touch the screen to take control again.
